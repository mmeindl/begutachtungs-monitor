# What happens to a Stellungnahme once it is filed?

**As of 2026-09-24.** Research memo answering one question: is anyone obliged
to read a Stellungnahme, to answer it or to say why it was not followed — in
the Begutachtung of a Ministerialentwurf, and later, when the Regierungsvorlage
or an Initiativantrag is before the Nationalrat? The monitor shows what became
of a draft; this memo records what the procedure itself promises a submitter,
which is the background to every "what became of the input" the pages show.

The quotes were fetched on 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-23/24 and are verbatim in
the original language. They were not re-read for this memo: check them against
the source before citing. The neighbouring questions are answered elsewhere
and not repeated here: whether a Begutachtung is required at all and which
reforms were proposed (`begutachtung-reformvorschlaege.md`), and how much
legislation never sees one (`begutachtung-uebersprungen.md`).

## Findings in four sentences

1. **Nobody is obliged to read, answer or explain.** Parliament publishes
   statements and passes them on; it does not process them.
2. **The one attempt at a duty to explain is a request, not a rule:** the
   Entschließung of 2017 asks ministries to say which suggestions they took up.
3. **Statements on a Regierungsvorlage reach the parliamentary clubs and the
   ministry, not the committee as a body.** In the one case traced here, the
   committee's changes were explained by the statements on the *draft* and by
   a hearing, and none of the statements on the Vorlage was mentioned.
4. **The chambers' statutory right to be consulted binds the ministries only.**
   A bill introduced by members of Parliament never triggers it.

## 1. No duty to process

Parliament's research service (RLW), Fachinfo *„Wie funktionieren
Begutachtungsverfahren zu Gesetzentwürfen?"* (03.02.2026), on what Parliament
does with the statements it publishes:

> Eine weitere Bearbeitung durch die Parlamentsdirektion erfolgt jedoch nicht.
> Das heißt, es werden weder Übersichten der Stellungnahmen noch
> Zusammenstellungen und Auswertungen der vorgebrachten Argumente erstellt.

and on who decides what is done with them:

> Es liegt in der Verantwortung der zuständigen Ministerien oder der
> Abgeordneten, welche Stellungnahmen sie aufgreifen und allenfalls weiter
> bearbeiten.

The only step towards a duty to explain is Entschließung 200/E of the
Nationalrat (2017), which asks the government to set out briefly in the
Regierungsvorlage which suggestions from the Begutachtung it followed. It is a
request. What became of it, and the sample of Regierungsvorlagen that do not
follow it, is in `begutachtung-reformvorschlaege.md` §2.

## 2. Statements on a Regierungsvorlage or an Initiativantrag

Since 01.08.2021 anyone can file a statement on an item before the Nationalrat
(§ 23b GOG-NR, as amended by BGBl. I 81/2024). The provision regulates
publication only. Parliament's own page „Stellung nehmen zu
Gesetzesinitiativen" says where they go:

> Freigegebene Stellungnahmen werden den parlamentarischen Klubs und dem
> zuständigen Bundesministerium für ihre Arbeit zur Verfügung gestellt

and they are published with the item. There is no committee procedure for
them and no duty attached (section 1). How many statements arrive through this
channel on laws that had no Begutachtung is measured in
`begutachtung-uebersprungen.md` §4c.

### One case followed through: the Informationsfreiheitsgesetz

95/ME (GP XXVII) became the Regierungsvorlage 2238 d.B.; ten statements were
filed on the Vorlage. The monitor's own comparison, measured 2026-09-23:

| Step | Units changed |
|---|---|
| Ministerialentwurf → Regierungsvorlage | 21 of 34 (1 inserted, 4 dropped) |
| Regierungsvorlage → Verfassungsausschuss (22.01.2024) | 12 of 35, 5 inserted |
| Ausschuss → Plenum (31.01.2024) | 2 |

The text changed considerably after the Vorlage. The committee report
(2420 d.B.) explains the changes with the „rund 200 Stellungnahmen" on the
draft („umfassend ausgewertet … zahlreiche Gespräche … Auf der Grundlage
dieser Ergebnisse wurde der Entwurf überarbeitet") and records a **hearing in
the committee** with Auskunftspersonen, among them representatives of
civil-society organisations. **None of the ten statements on the Vorlage is
mentioned.** In this case influence after the Vorlage ran through the hearing,
not through the web form.

One case proves no rule. What it shows is where to look: hearings and their
Auskunftspersonen are recorded in the committee report.

### What cannot be measured

Which weight a statement on a Vorlage has is a causal question nobody can
answer from the data. A base rate would be measurable (Vorlagen with and
without statements, changed in committee or not). It was deliberately not
measured: the RLW Fachinfo *„Wie haben sich Gesetzesinitiativen in der XXVII.
GP verändert?"* (31.10.2024) already finds that only just under 40 % of
initiatives are passed unchanged — committee changes are frequent with or
without statements, and contested bills attract both. The correlation would be
weak evidence presented as an effect, which the monitor's framing rule forbids
(`CLAUDE.md`, "Framing").

## 3. The chambers' right to be consulted does not follow the bill

§ 93 Arbeiterkammergesetz, § 66c Ärztegesetz and § 10 Wirtschaftskammergesetz
give these chambers a statutory right to receive drafts and to comment. The
RLW Fachinfo of 03.02.2026 names all three as obligations **of the federal
ministries**. A bill that members of Parliament introduce as an Initiativantrag
does not come from a ministry, so the obligation never arises; the same page
says that drafts „die nicht von der Bundesregierung sondern von Abgeordneten
ausgehen" are not sent out for Begutachtung, and names § 23b GOG-NR as the
Nationalrat's answer to that criticism.

Two consequences for wording, on the site and anywhere else:

- "Bypasses a legal duty" is wrong. The duty is not bypassed; on the
  parliamentary route it does not exist. The accurate sentence is *ohne
  Begutachtung*.
- "Never publicly consulted" is wrong as well, because of § 23b. What is
  missing without a Begutachtung is a deadline, an invited circle and a
  ministry that receives the statements.

**Not verified:** the three provisions were not read in the RIS; the statement
above rests on the RLW's summary. Read the norm text before relying on the
detail.

**Nachtrag 14.11.2026: AKG and WKG read.** `wege-zu-einer-rechtsnorm.md`
(„Begutachtung vor dem Parlament: die Rechtslage") read both in the RIS.
Each binds more than the federal ministries: § 93 Abs. 2 AKG requires
„Entwürfe von Gesetzen … vor ihrer Einbringung in die jeweilige gesetzgebende
Körperschaft" to be sent to the competent Arbeiterkammer, „das gleiche gilt
für Verordnungen und Kundmachungen" — Bund and Länder alike; § 10 Abs. 1 WKG
extends the chambers' „Begutachtungsrecht" to Verordnungen „einschließlich
solcher aus dem Bereich der mittelbaren Bundesverwaltung", to
Staatsverträge and to Vereinbarungen gemäß Art. 15a B-VG. Both oblige the
sender to transmit, not to answer. The RLW's „obligations of the federal
ministries" above is therefore narrower than the norm text; the
consequences for wording drawn above are not changed by it. § 66c
Ärztegesetz is still unread.
